Open PDF and EPUB content of media items in a full-screen reader on the details page. The reader code loads only when a visitor opens a file, and PDF and EPUB support load separately. The reader offers page navigation and a progress bar to jump to any point in the book. PDF files show the total number of pages and can be zoomed. EPUB files get a table of contents, adjustable text size, and a dark mode.
