Add `overrides.footer` to replace the footer component. Deprecate `footerComponent` while continuing to support it: its value is copied to `overrides.footer` unless the new setting is provided.

Add `injects.head` to inject a component into the HTML head. Deprecate `headComponent` while continuing to support it: its value is copied to `injects.head` unless the new setting is provided.
